About Us

Numbers you can
trust. Every time.

Hazz Bookkeeping Services is an Atlantic Canada-based bookkeeping firm dedicated to helping non-profit organizations maintain clean, accurate financial records — without the overhead of a full-time accountant.

We understand the unique financial landscape of non-profits: restricted funds, grant reporting requirements, and the need for transparency to boards and donors. With a commerce background in Accounting and Marketing and a CPA designation in progress, we bring both rigour and a genuine commitment to the sector.
